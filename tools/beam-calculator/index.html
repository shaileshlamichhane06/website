How it works

For a centered point load, each reaction is P/2 and maximum moment is PL/4. For a uniform load, each reaction is wL/2 and maximum moment is wL²/8. Deflection combines PL³/(48EI) and 5wL⁴/(384EI).

Assumptions and limitations

Simply supported, prismatic, linearly elastic beam; centered point load; full-span uniform load; small deflection. This is not a design check.

Worked example

A 10 ft beam with a 2 kip centered load has 1 kip reactions and a 5 kip-ft maximum moment.
