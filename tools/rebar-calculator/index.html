How it works

Total length equals quantity × bar length. Total weight equals total length × selected nominal unit weight.

Assumptions and limitations

Weights are nominal ASTM A615 values and exclude hooks, laps, couplers, fabrication tolerances, coatings, waste, and delivery weight.

Worked example

Ten #4 bars at 20 ft each total 200 ft. At 0.668 lb/ft, estimated total weight is 133.6 lb.

Reference data: Nominal weights from ASTM A615/A615M, Table 1. For estimation and education only.
