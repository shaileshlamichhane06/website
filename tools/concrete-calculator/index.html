How it works

Rectangular shapes use length × width × depth. Round columns use π(d/2)² × height. Waste is applied after the net volume.

Assumptions and limitations

Nominal bag yields are 0.45 ft³ for 60 lb bags and 0.60 ft³ for 80 lb bags. Confirm actual product yield and supplier ordering requirements.

Worked example

A 10 ft × 10 ft slab at 4 in thick is 33.33 ft³, or 1.23 yd³ before waste.
